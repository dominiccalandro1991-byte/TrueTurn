# TrueTurn Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 4 September 2026  
**Operator:** BackRoad Inc.  
**Contact:** the GitHub repository owner for this project

TrueTurn is a virtual dice and card club. Tokens and Diamonds are not money and cannot be withdrawn.

## What we store on your device

- A guest player id
- Display name
- Luck phrase (client seed)
- Local wallet balances for Tokens and Diamonds

These stay on the device unless you connect to a hosted club server you control.

## What a hosted server may store

If you (or we) run the club API:

- Match records, seed commitments, and action logs needed to verify fairness
- Ledger entries for virtual chips
- Optional avatar job status

We do not collect government ID, payment cards, or biometric templates in this build. Avatar upload is optional, consent-gated, and mocked unless an image provider is configured.

## What we do not do

- No real-money deposits or cash-out
- No sale of personal data
- No third-party advertising SDK in this build
- No tracking pixels required to play

## Children

TrueTurn is not directed at children under 13.

## Deletion

Clear the app’s local storage or uninstall. Hosted match logs, if any, can be deleted by the operator on request.

## Changes

Updates will be posted in this file and in the store listing “What’s New”.
