## Description

TrueTurn is a server-authoritative club for eight classic tables: 10,000 (Farkle), Ship Captain Crew, Craps Pass Line, Texas Hold’em, Spades, Hearts, Pitch, and single-deck Pinochle.

Each outcome is derived from HMAC-SHA256 with a published server-seed commitment. After a match you can verify the roll or shuffle yourself. Tokens and Diamonds are non-withdrawable club chips. There is no cash-out, exchange, or real-money gambling.

House bots sit empty seats so a table always plays. Guest play works immediately.
