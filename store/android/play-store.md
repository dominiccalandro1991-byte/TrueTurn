## Full description

TrueTurn is a private club for eight deterministic tables: 10,000, Ship Captain Crew, Craps, Texas Hold’em, Spades, Hearts, Pitch, and Pinochle.

Every roll and shuffle is committed with HMAC-SHA256 before it lands. Verify any finished match from the Verify tab. Tokens and Diamonds are virtual and non-withdrawable. This is not real-money gambling.
